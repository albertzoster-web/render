How Does an Interior Designer Plan a Kitchen Remodel in Chicago?

A successful kitchen starts long before construction begins. Good planning connects your lifestyle, available space, materials, lighting, storage, budget, and construction requirements into one practical design.

The Short Answer

Kitchen planning begins with how the space needs to work.

The best kitchen design is not simply about choosing attractive cabinets or countertops. It begins by understanding how people move through the room, where they prepare food, how much storage they need, and how the kitchen connects with the rest of the home.

An interior designer can bring these decisions together before construction starts. This helps homeowners make important choices earlier and reduces the chance of expensive changes later.

What should be planned first?

Start with the kitchen's purpose, layout, storage requirements, appliance locations, lighting, materials, and overall budget.

Once these fundamentals are clear, finishes and decorative details become much easier to choose.

Planning Process

Six decisions shape a successful kitchen.

01

Understand the Lifestyle

Cooking habits, entertaining, family routines, and daily traffic help determine what the kitchen needs to accomplish.

02

Study the Layout

The location of counters, appliances, islands, doors, and walkways affects how efficiently the room functions.

03

Plan Storage

Cabinet organization, pantry space, drawers, and specialty storage can make a major difference in everyday usability.

04

Select Materials

Countertops, cabinetry, flooring, backsplashes, and hardware should work together visually and practically.

05

Plan Lighting

Ambient, task, and accent lighting can make the room easier to use while creating a more comfortable atmosphere.

06

Prepare for Construction

Permits, measurements, demolition, plumbing, electrical work, finishes, inspections, and scheduling need coordination.

Layout First

Beautiful kitchens still need to function efficiently.

Layout is one of the most important parts of planning. A visually impressive kitchen can become frustrating if the refrigerator, sink, cooking area, island, and storage are poorly positioned.

  • Comfortable movement between work zones
  • Practical appliance placement
  • Enough countertop workspace
  • Storage that matches daily routines
  • Clearances around islands and walkways
  • Connection with adjoining rooms

FAQ

Common kitchen planning questions

What does an interior designer consider first?

The first considerations usually include the homeowner's lifestyle, existing layout, circulation, storage, appliances, lighting, materials, budget, and project goals.

Should layout be decided before materials?

Yes. Layout and functionality should generally be established before final material selections because cabinet dimensions, appliance locations, islands, and work zones influence many later decisions.

How important is lighting in a kitchen?

Very important. A good lighting plan combines general lighting with task lighting and, where appropriate, accent lighting to support both practical work and the overall atmosphere.

Can an existing Chicago kitchen layout be changed?

Often, yes, although the possibilities depend on structural conditions, plumbing, electrical systems, windows, doors, ventilation, and applicable construction requirements.
